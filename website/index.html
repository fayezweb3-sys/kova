Shortage forecast

Most reports never reach your shelf. Kova shows which ones will.

Kova scores each product's risk of a real shortage over the next 90 days, using the factors researchers have linked to shortages in Canada and every report filed since 2017.

The model is trained on reports up to 2024 and tested on 2025–26 before any score is shown to a pharmacy. Scores come with the reasons behind them, never as a bare number.

Our principles

Official data, shown honestly.

Kova is only useful if you can trust it at the counter, so every answer shows where it came from.

  • Every answer has a source and a dateEach status links back to the government record it came from, with when Kova last checked it.
  • The pharmacist decidesKova lists products the province already treats as interchangeable. Therapeutic substitutions stay a professional decision.
  • No patient dataKova works on products, not people. A watchlist is a list of DINs.
  • Works next to your pharmacy systemNothing to replace or integrate. Look it up in Kova, dispense in the system you use today.
